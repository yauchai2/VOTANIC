Alan 2026-11-16 (重要): avatar colours and behaviour colours are for special cases only. Do not use them for illustrations, empty-state art or general decoration.

**Why:** they carry identity meaning (avatars; Behaviour node/variable kinds such as the ochre project-variable), so decorative use dilutes it.
**How to apply:** empty-state art and decorative graphics use neutral tokens (text/secondary outlines) plus at most one text/accent. behaviour/project-variable stays only where it marks Project variables. RT / Empty art was recoloured on this basis (avatar tokens removed). Related: [[xrcc-studio-feel-direction]], [[xrcc-instance-vs-project-variables]].
