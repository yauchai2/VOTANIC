Alan (2026-10-09, marked 重要): "我需要有美感和專業的UI/UX, 介面要有趣而非一般AI味, 我要有人味". All UI built for Votanic/XRCC should be aesthetically refined and professional, with character and warmth, and must not look like generic AI/template output.

**Why:** Votanic is a product company and Alan is the UI/UX designer; templated-looking screens (uniform cards, default purple-gradient feel, filler copy) reflect badly.
**How to apply:** Stay inside the Library tokens and components, but craft deliberately: clear hierarchy and rhythm, purposeful emphasis (e.g. bold title / primary vs secondary action), considered spacing and alignment, realistic specific content in natural Hong Kong Cantonese/Traditional Chinese instead of lorem or generic phrases, thoughtful empty/error/loading states, small human touches in microcopy. Avoid decorative gimmicks that break the design system. Self-review screenshots critically before delivering; say what is a deliberate design choice.
