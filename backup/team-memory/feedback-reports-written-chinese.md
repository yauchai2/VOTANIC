Alan (2026-11-02, Voyage 360 Loading thread): "匯報也請用書面語". Reports and replies to him should use 書面語 (written Chinese), still Traditional Chinese with full-width punctuation.

**Why:** colloquial Cantonese reads informal in design reports he may share or present.
**How to apply:** Use 書面語 wording (例如「已完成」「沒有」「是否」), avoid 嘅/咗/冇/唔/係/啲. Keep short. Related: [[punctuation-rule-zh-en]].
