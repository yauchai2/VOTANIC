Alan (2026-10-14): 英文 "," 後加空格；中文標點用全形。例：English `A, B, C! A - B; C`；中文 `中文，中文。中文；中文！`。「記底，所有地方都應用」。

**Why:** consistent, professional text across docs, UI copy and replies.
**How to apply:** In every output (reports, talking points, UI strings, replies to Alan, Figma text), use full-width ， 。 ； ： ！ （ ） in Chinese sentences and half-width punctuation plus a following space in English. Do not break numbers like 12,000 or code/paths. Related: [[ui-copy-english-first]].
